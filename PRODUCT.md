# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo developers who create, review, publish, and archive HTML plans from the CLI and dashboard. They are working at a desk with a code editor and browser open, and need to scan status quickly without losing control of the underlying HTML.

## Product Purpose

HTML Planner stores HTML plans, groups them by project, and makes review, approval, publishing, preview, and archival workflows easy to manage. Success means a developer can move from a newly generated plan to a reviewed, shareable URL with minimal friction and clear state at every step.

## Brand Personality

Bold, exact, technical. The interface should feel like a dependable command center: direct, legible, and confident without becoming cold or theatrical. Use Encase's tactical containment with a restrained accent for state and focus.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, soft gradient-heavy layouts, decorative glassmorphism, weak gray-on-gray contrast, and dense control clusters that hide the primary workflow. Do not make the product feel like a marketing landing page or a toy terminal mockup.

## Design Principles

- Make plan state unmistakable at a glance.
- Keep the review workflow close to the plan it changes.
- Treat the HTML preview as a first-class output, not a secondary detail.
- Prefer explicit controls and honest labels over clever abstraction.
- Give solo developers a fast path while keeping room for team review later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must be keyboard accessible with visible focus, readable at increased text sizes, and usable without relying on color alone. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve semantic structure for assistive technology.
